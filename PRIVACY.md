# Privacy Policy

LoL Schedule does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to the developer or any third party.

The extension:
- Retrieves public match schedules from Riot Games' LoL Esports API.
- Retrieves public profile and live status for the Twitch channels you add, from Twitch.
- Stores your settings locally using Chrome's storage API: your list of Twitch channel names, the badge and notification toggles, the league filter, and a cached copy of the public schedule.
- Shows desktop notifications (if you enable them) when a tracked streamer goes live.

Your streamer list never leaves your browser except as channel names sent to Twitch to look up their public live status.

The extension does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or cookies.
